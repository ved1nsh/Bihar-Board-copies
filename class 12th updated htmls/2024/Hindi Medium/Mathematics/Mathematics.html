उत्तर

$P_{1}=x-2 y+2 z-6=0$

$$ \begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow \quad 3 x-6 y+6 z-18=0 \\ & P_{2}=3 x-6 y+6 z-2=0 \\ & a=3, b=-6, c=6 ; \quad d_{1}=-18, d_{2}=-2 \end{aligned} $$
$$ \begin{aligned} \text { दूरी (distance) } & =\left|\frac{d_{2}-d_{1}}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}}\right|=\left|\frac{-2+18}{\sqrt{9+36+36}}\right|=\left|\frac{16}{\sqrt{81}}\right| \\ & =\left|\frac{16}{9}\right|=\frac{16}{9} \text { इकाई; Ans. } \end{aligned} $$
