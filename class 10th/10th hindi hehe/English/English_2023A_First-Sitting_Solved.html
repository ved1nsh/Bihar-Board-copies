An Ideal Family
उत्तर

An ideal family leads a peaceful, happy and prosperous life. Everybody in the family realizes his responsibilities and performs his duties. They maintain cordial and friendly relations with their neighbours and with those who come in their contact.

In an ideal family everybody maintains courtesy and discipline, showing due affection and respect to one another. The children are obedient, well-mannered and smart, while the elders take all possible care of the family. Every member feels proud of the family.
