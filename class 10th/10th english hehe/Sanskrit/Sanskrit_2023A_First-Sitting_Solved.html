Which foreign travellers have described Patna in their memoir-books ?
Answer

Foreign travellers such as Megasthenes, Faxian (Fa-Hien), Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang), I-tsing and others have described Patna in their memoir-books.

Q.
5 (घ)
2 marks
Which South Indian Sanskrit female writers are famous for their stray (sphuṭa) verses ?
Answer

Queen Vijaya-bhattarika of the Chalukya dynasty composed verses under the name Vijayanka and contributed to classical (laukika) Sanskrit literature. About forty South Indian women composed nearly one hundred and fifty Sanskrit poems. Among them, Gangadevi, Tirumalamba, Shila-bhattarika, Devakumarika, Rambhadramba and others are prominent.

What is the result of attaining a desired object through undesirable means ?
Answer

Those who obtain a desired (iṣṭa, good) thing through undesirable (aniṣṭa, bad) means do not attain a good end, because even by drinking poisoned nectar (amṛta), only death is obtained.

What was the special quality of Karna's armour and earrings (kavach-kundal) ?
Answer

Karna's protection was contained in the armour (kavach) and earrings (kundal) that were attached to his body. As long as the armour and earrings were on Karna's body, no one could kill him.

What is śāstra (scripture) ?
Answer

The instruction given to human beings, through works composed by men, regarding attachment or detachment in worldly matters, is called śāstra (scripture).
