For what value of k are the points (1,1), (3,k) and (−1,4) collinear?
Answer

Since the three points are collinear, the area of the triangle = 0.

$$\tfrac{1}{2}\left[1(k-4)+3(4-1)+(-1)(1-k)\right]=0$$
$$\Rightarrow 2k+4=0 \;\Rightarrow\; k=-2$$

Therefore k = −2 (Answer)

If cos A = 4/5, find the value of cot A + sin A.
Answer
$$\cos A=\frac{4}{5}=\frac{b}{h}\Rightarrow b=4k,\; h=5k$$
$$p=\sqrt{h^2-b^2}=\sqrt{25k^2-16k^2}=3k$$
$$\cot A+\sin A=\frac{b}{p}+\frac{p}{h}=\frac{4}{3}+\frac{3}{5}=\frac{20+9}{15}=\frac{29}{15}$$

Answer: 29/15

Prove that 5 − 2√3 is not a rational number.
Answer

On the contrary, assume that 5 − 2√3 is rational, that is,

$$5-2\sqrt{3}=\frac{a}{b}\;\Rightarrow\; \sqrt{3}=\frac{5}{2}-\frac{a}{2b}$$

The right-hand side is a rational number, so √3 must also be rational. But √3 is irrational — this is a contradiction. Therefore 5 − 2√3 is not a rational number. (Proved)

The area of a rectangle is 528 m². Its length is 1 metre more than twice its breadth. Find the length and breadth of the rectangle.
Answer

Let breadth = x m, then length = (2x+1) m.

$$(2x+1)x=528\Rightarrow 2x^2+x-528=0$$
$$2x^2+33x-32x-528=0\Rightarrow (x-16)(2x+33)=0$$
$$x=16 \quad\text{(since breadth cannot be negative)}$$

Breadth = 16 m, Length = (2×16+1) = 33 m

Prove that the ratio of the perimeters of two similar triangles is equal to the ratio of their corresponding sides.
Answer

Given: △ABC ~ △DEF

$$\frac{AB}{DE}=\frac{BC}{EF}=\frac{AC}{DF}=k$$
$$\Rightarrow AB=pk,\; BC=qk,\; AC=rk$$
$$\frac{\text{Perimeter of △ABC}}{\text{Perimeter of △DEF}}=\frac{pk+qk+rk}{p+q+r}=\frac{k(p+q+r)}{p+q+r}=k$$

Hence the ratio of perimeters is equal to the ratio of corresponding sides. (Proved)
