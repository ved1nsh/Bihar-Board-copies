Gist / English paraphrase — True enthusiasm (utsah) is that which motivates a person to act. The joy a person feels when they resolve to remove someone's suffering is especially inspiring. The capacity to bear hardship, sorrow or loss in order to complete a task — and the joy that arises from bearing all this — is what we call enthusiasm. Enthusiasm is a mixture of joy and courage.

(i) What is true enthusiasm (सच्चा उत्साह)?
True enthusiasm is that which motivates a person to take action.

(ii) What kind of joy is inspiring?
When a person resolves to remove someone's suffering, the joy they then experience is especially inspiring.

(iii) Who possesses special courage?
A person who has firm determination to accomplish a task possesses special courage.

(iv) What kind of joy is called enthusiasm?
The joy that arises while doing a task — by bearing hardship, sorrow or loss — is called enthusiasm.

(v) Title : The importance of enthusiasm in life.

Write an essay on any one of the given topics. (1 × 10 = 10) — 'Student Life' is solved below.

10 marks
Student Life (विद्यार्थी जीवन)
Answer

Introduction : Student life is the most important part of our life. During this period, a student goes to school or college in search of knowledge and learns many things from books. In truth, student life is a time for learning.

Entry into school : Children are enrolled in school at the age of four or five. From this age children formally begin their studies, and at the same time they also receive informal education.

Classmates : Classmates play a major role in student life. By exchanging knowledge with classmates, children strengthen their own knowledge. When tired of studying, they refresh themselves by playing with their classmates.

Gratitude towards teachers : It is the teachers who instil in children the habits of obedience, virtue and discipline. Therefore, a student should always feel grateful to the teacher.

Conclusion : In this way, student life is a time of preparation, character-building and the gaining of education. Truly, student life is the golden time of a person's life.

Which problem does the writer Bhimrao Ambedkar consider an even bigger evil in today's industries than poverty and oppression, and why?
Answer

The writer considers the caste system (jaati-pratha) an even bigger problem than poverty and oppression in today's industries. The main reason is that the caste system does not care about a person's interest and aptitude. A person cannot choose a profession according to his own capability, and because of this dislike his ability begins to decline. From an economic point of view also, the caste system is dangerous.

2 marks
What does the poet Premghan have to say about the city, the marketplace and the economy?
Answer

The poet says that during British rule, Indian industries were ruined and the use of indigenous (swadeshi) goods came to an end, because hand-made goods were more expensive than machine-made ones. As a result, foreign goods dominated the markets, craftsmen lost their jobs, and our economy fell into an extremely pitiful state.

Q.
5 (viii)
2 marks
From the viewpoint of the poet Sumitranandan Pant, why is the penance and self-restraint (tap-sanyam) of Mother India successful today?
Answer

Through the success of penance and self-restraint, the poet is pointing to Mahatma Gandhi's truth and non-violence (satya-ahimsa). Gandhiji, by offering people the nectar of non-violence, removed the fear and ignorance from within them and gave the people of the country such confidence that, by uniting, they could destroy even the greatest power. For this reason the penance and self-restraint of Mother India is regarded as successful.

Explain the meaning of: "मेरा धर्म कैदखाने का धर्म नहीं है।" ("My religion is not a prison-house religion.")
Answer

Through this line, the writer advises us to understand the importance of our own culture. The writer says that before understanding the value of another culture, one should try to understand the value of one's own culture. A person or a nation can only be proud when it expresses its highest ideas. The writer wishes that people of every culture should come here and move freely, but we should not forget our own language or culture — because his religion is not the religion of a prison, but the religion of freedom and excellence.
